# SIH pitch deck outline — Advanced NE-Route AI

1. **Title:** NE-Route AI — Intelligent Logistics & Accessibility Command Center for North East India.
2. **Problem:** remote communities and essential shipments face cascading disruptions; field reports, road access and logistics decisions are fragmented.
3. **Users:** government officials, disaster response teams, logistics managers, field officers and public reporters.
4. **Product:** a geospatial command center that observes conditions, predicts disruption exposure, optimizes routes and coordinates response.
5. **Digital Twin:** compound-hazard scenario inputs → network fragmentation → shipment exposure → district accessibility loss → illustrative recovery worklist and resilience curve.
6. **Strategic Relief Staging:** multiple regional depots, demand-prioritized district coverage, risk-adjusted travel time, stock shortfall and restock recommendations.
7. **Incident Signal Fusion:** spatial-temporal clustering and source corroboration to identify potentially related reports while preserving human verification.
8. **Decision Governance:** SHA-256 chained proposal / review journal, independent authorized review and integrity verification.
9. **AI and routing architecture:** React / Leaflet, FastAPI, PostgreSQL / PostGIS, NetworkX routing and a scikit-learn Random Forest prototype trained on synthetic labels.
10. **Responsible AI and safety:** explicit synthetic-data labeling, model provenance, confidence and freshness indicators, non-mutating simulations, no autonomous dispatch and no auto-merge of incident reports.
11. **Impact measurement plan:** time from report to verification, time to identify a viable alternative, critical cargo exposure duration, depot coverage, shortage volume, independent-review latency and audit-chain integrity. Report measured results only after a real pilot.
12. **Scalability:** integrate authorized state GIS / road data, weather observations, agency incident feeds, inventory systems and offline field collection.
13. **Governance and sustainability:** agency data ownership, role-based access, operator training, source attribution, audit retention and clear service ownership.
14. **Roadmap:** validate baseline topology and labels → pilot selected corridors → connect official feeds → assess model calibration / fairness → expand states and agencies.
